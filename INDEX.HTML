Kartik Amavasya • Ayodhya Aagman अयोध्या नगरी में श्री राम आगमन

The Festival of Immortal Light

Deepawali celebrates the joyous return of Maryada Purushottam Prabhu Shri Ram, Mata Sita, and Lakshman Ji to sacred Ayodhya after fourteen years of exile and the victory of Dharma over arrogance. On the moonless night of Kartik Amavasya, the citizens of Ayodhya illuminated every threshold with millions of earthen diyas, transforming darkness into celestial radiance.

This sacred night also marks the auspicious appearance of Goddess Mahalakshmi from the cosmic ocean of milk (Samudra Manthan) alongside Lord Ganesha—bestowing wisdom, harmony, auspiciousness (Shubh), and spiritual abundance (Labh) upon every devoted home.

"O mind, sing the glory of compassionate Lord Ramachandra, who dispels the fierce fear of worldly existence; whose eyes, face, hands, and feet resemble freshly bloomed crimson lotuses."
